## What TuStack is

TuStack is a search engine for finding out which companies in Spain work with each technology, based on the job offers they publish.

Access is free and open. It is not a job board: we do not take part in hiring, we receive no applications, and we have no relationship with the companies listed in the catalogue.

## Where the information comes from, and how accurate it is

The catalogue is built automatically from offers published on third party boards (Adzuna, Jooble and Tecnoempleo) and from public information about the companies. Some descriptions are generated with artificial intelligence tools.

That means **the information may contain errors, be incomplete or have gone out of date**. We offer it for information only and do not guarantee it is accurate. Before making any decision, check the data at its source: every offer links to the board that published it.

Offers are not deleted when they close, because the history is what shows which technologies a company has used over time. That is why active and historical offers are shown separately.

If you are a company and spot a mistake on your page, or want it removed, write to us and we will sort it out.
