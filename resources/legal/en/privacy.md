TuStack is run from Spain and this policy follows the GDPR and Spanish data protection law.

## What we handle and what for

TuStack works without an account. We only handle personal data if you choose to register or if you write to us.

### If you register

| Data | What for | Legal basis | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address and name | Creating and keeping your account | Performance of a contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR) | Until you delete the account |
| Password, hashed and unreadable to us | Checking it is you when you sign in | Performance of a contract | Until you delete the account |
| Saved companies and saved searches | Providing the feature you asked for | Performance of a contract | Until you delete them or the account |
| Email address, for new offer alerts | Sending the alerts you turned on | Consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR) | Until you turn the alerts off |
| Telegram id, if you connect the bot | Sending alerts over Telegram | Consent | Until you disconnect the bot |

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, they give us your name and email address. We never receive or store your password.

### If you write to us

We handle your email address and the content of your message for the sole purpose of replying, on legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR). We keep the conversation as long as it takes to help you.

### If you only browse

We use no analytics or advertising cookies, which is why you will see no cookie banner. See the [cookie policy](/en/cookies).

We measure use of the site anonymously with **Umami**, hosted on our own server. It sets no cookies, creates no identifier and cannot recognise you or follow you across sites. The data is aggregated: page views, country, device type and referring page.

We also keep an anonymous record of searches: the technology and the province looked for, with the date. **No ip address, no browser, no identifier of any kind**, so that record cannot be tied back to you. It tells us which technologies people care about so we can improve the catalogue.

## Company and job offer data

The TuStack catalogue is built from job offers published by third parties (Adzuna, Jooble and Tecnoempleo) and from public information about companies.

This is information about companies rather than people, and we handle it on legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR) in order to offer a company search by technology. If an offer happened to contain a contact person's details, or if you represent a company and want its page removed, write to us and we will do it.

To fill in company information we use the **Google Gemini** api, and **Google Translate** for descriptions. We send them only the company name and the public content of its offers, never user data.

## International transfers

Resend, Telegram and Google are outside the European Economic Area. Those transfers rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, or on whichever adequacy framework applies.

## Your rights

At any time you may exercise your rights of **access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability**, and withdraw your consent where the processing rests on it.

To do so, write to **info@tustack.es** saying which right you want to exercise. We will answer within one month at the latest. We may ask for proof of identity.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you may complain to the **Spanish Data Protection Agency** (www.aepd.es), C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid.

## Deleting your account

Write to us and we will delete your account, your saved companies and your saved searches. Deletion is permanent.

## Children

TuStack is not aimed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Security

The site runs entirely over HTTPS. Passwords, where they exist, are stored hashed and we cannot read them. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information, although no system can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page and update its date. If a change materially affects your data we will tell you by email.
